STTOS

Stories That Transform OS · made by Into the Light Creative

STTOS is a desktop app that helps churches and nonprofits make story films: from the first brief to the finished film and how it landed. It runs on the organization's own Mac.

Why STTOS asks to connect to Google

When an organization connects its YouTube channel, STTOS shows that channel's numbers beside each project (views, watch time, the films that did best) and, only when someone presses Publish to YouTube, uploads a finished film as unlisted. If the organization also chooses mail, calendar or files, STTOS reads and drafts them for the person on that Mac; it never sends anything without their yes.

Each organization signs in through its own Google app, made in its own Google account. Nothing passes through a server run by STTOS.
