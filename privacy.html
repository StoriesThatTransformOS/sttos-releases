Privacy policy

STTOS (Stories That Transform OS) · updated 14 October 2026

STTOS is a desktop app that runs on your organization's own Mac. This page says what it does with information from your Google account when you connect it.

What STTOS reads

Only what you allow on Google's consent screen. For YouTube: your channel's details and its analytics, so each project can show how its film is doing, and uploading a film when you press Publish to YouTube. If you also connect Gmail, Calendar or Drive: the messages, events and files STTOS needs to show you who is waiting on you, the week's events, and the documents you work on.

Where it is kept

On your Mac, in your organization's own folders. The sign-in key Google gives STTOS is kept in your Mac's Keychain. There is no STTOS server: your Google data is not sent to Into the Light Creative or to anyone else by STTOS.

AI services

When you ask STTOS to write or check something, the text it needs may be sent to the AI service your organization has chosen (such as Anthropic or OpenAI), under that service's terms, to do what you asked. Google user data is never used to train AI models, and it is never sold, rented or used for advertising.

Google API Services

STTOS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Remove STTOS's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting your organization's STTOS folders deletes what STTOS kept.

Contact

Questions about this policy: Into the Light Creative, makers of STTOS. Questions about your organization's own data: your organization.
